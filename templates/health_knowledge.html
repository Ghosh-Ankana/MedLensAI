    When To Seek Care
    SymPredAl is designed to help users better understand health information, but it is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If your symptoms are severe, worsening, persistent, or causing concern, you should consult a qualified healthcare professional. Seek immediate medical attention for symptoms such as difficulty breathing, chest pain, severe allergic reactions, loss of consciousness, confusion, signs of stroke, or any other potentially life-threatening condition. Even when symptoms appear mild, a healthcare provider is the best source of guidance when you are uncertain about your health. SymPredAI should be used as an educational resource that supports learning and informed conversations with healthcare professionals, not as a tool for making medical decisions on its own.
    Understanding The Machine Learning Predictions
    XGBoost makes predictions by identifying patterns between symptoms and diseases that it learned from the training data. When a user selects symptoms, the model analyzes those symptoms and compares them with the patterns it has learned, then assigns a prediction based on how closely they match each disease. The prediction should not be interpreted as a definite diagnosis, since the model can make mistakes and its accuracy depends on the quality and amount of training data. Instead, the results provide an estimate of which disease best matches the symptoms according to the patterns learned by the model. Unlike a traditional decision tree that may use Gini impurity or entropy to determine splits, XGBoost evaluates potential splits using gain derived from the gradients and second-order gradients of its objective function. For multi-class disease classification, the objective is based on multi-class log loss. This allows each successive tree to focus on improving the errors made by previous trees while regularization helps prevent overfitting.
